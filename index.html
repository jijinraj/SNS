Before booking

A few things to know before we arrive.

Electricity access

The customer must provide safe access to a standard household electricity supply during the appointment.

Safe parking

The vehicle must be parked somewhere the service can be carried out safely and legally.

Personal belongings

Please remove valuables, important documents and personal belongings before the appointment so accessible areas can be cleaned properly.

Child seats + booster seats

Child seats and booster seats will not normally be removed, adjusted or reinstalled as part of the Quick Clean.

Very dirty vehicles

Heavy pet hair, excessive dirt, sand, mud or unusually dirty interiors can take longer than a normal Quick Clean. If an additional charge is needed, it will be discussed and agreed before the work starts.
