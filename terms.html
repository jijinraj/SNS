Terms & Conditions

Please read the following terms before submitting a booking request.

  1. The £15 Quick Clean is a basic maintenance clean and not a full/deep valet.
  2. The service includes vacuuming of seats, floors and mats, plus a dashboard and centre-console wipe.
  3. Boot cleaning is an additional £5.
  4. Heavy pet hair, excessive dirt, sand, mud or unusually dirty vehicles may require additional time or cost. Any extra charge will be agreed before work starts.
  5. The customer must provide safe access to a standard household electricity supply.
  6. The vehicle must be parked somewhere the service can be carried out safely and legally.
  7. Please remove valuables, important documents and personal belongings before the appointment.
  8. Child seats and booster seats will not normally be removed, adjusted or reinstalled by Suck N' Shine. If you would like the area underneath cleaned, please remove the seat before the appointment.
  9. Please remove or secure pet carriers, dog guards and other large removable accessories if you want the areas underneath them cleaned.
  10. Areas covered by personal belongings, boxes, shopping, luggage or other items may be left uncleaned if the customer has not removed them.
  11. Locked gloveboxes, storage compartments and other closed personal-storage areas will not be opened unless specifically requested by the customer.
  12. Please tell us about any loose, damaged or fragile trim, aftermarket electronics, accessories or existing interior damage before cleaning begins.
  13. Extra care will be taken around screens, controls and electronics; liquids will not be heavily applied to these areas.
  14. The Quick Clean does not guarantee complete removal of stains, odours, pet hair or deeply embedded dirt.
  15. We may refuse or stop a job involving mould, bodily fluids, needles/sharps, hazardous substances, pests or other potentially unsafe contamination.
  16. Obvious rubbish may be removed, but items that could reasonably be personal property will not be thrown away without confirmation.
  17. Seats may be moved forwards or backwards where necessary to vacuum accessible areas, but seats, trim panels or interior fittings will not be dismantled or removed.
  18. Before/after photographs will only be used for marketing or social media with the customer's permission.
  19. Submitting this form is a booking request only. Your appointment is not confirmed until Suck N' Shine contacts you.
  20. Appointments may need to be rearranged if weather or other conditions make the service unsafe or impractical.
  21. Payment is due once the agreed service has been completed.
  22. Your contact and booking information will be used to manage your booking and communicate with you about the service.
